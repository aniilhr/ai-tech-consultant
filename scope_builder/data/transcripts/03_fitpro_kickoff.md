Anil (Consultant): Hi Jordan, thanks for joining. Tell me about FitPro Coaches and what you'd like to build.

Jordan (Client - Founder): We're a marketplace for certified personal trainers. People come to us looking for a trainer, and right now we match them manually through a Google Form. It doesn't scale. We need a website where trainers have profiles and clients can find them.

Anil (Consultant): Who pays, the trainers or the clients?

Jordan (Client - Founder): Trainers pay a monthly membership to receive leads. Clients are free.

Anil (Consultant): How should a lead reach a trainer?

Jordan (Client - Founder): A client fills out a short form: their goal, like weight loss or strength, their zip code, and whether they want in-person or online sessions. Then the lead should go to up to three trainers near that zip code who match the goal.

Anil (Consultant): So lead matching by location and specialty, sent to a limited number of trainers. Should trainers be able to accept or decline?

Jordan (Client - Founder): Yes, ideally they accept the lead in their dashboard, and then they see the client's contact details.

Anil (Consultant): Okay. What's on the trainer profile?

Jordan (Client - Founder): Photo, bio, certifications, specialties, price range per session, location, and whether they do online sessions. Plus reviews from clients.

Anil (Consultant): Do you want trainers to post content?

Jordan (Client - Founder): Yes, a blog where trainers can publish articles, and an events calendar for bootcamps and workshops. That's great for SEO.

Anil (Consultant): Membership plans for trainers, how many?

Jordan (Client - Founder): Let's go with three: Starter, Pro, and Elite. More leads per month on higher plans. We decided that last week.

Anil (Consultant): Payments through which provider?

Jordan (Client - Founder): We don't have one yet. Whatever is easiest. Probably Stripe.

Anil (Consultant): We'll assume Stripe unless you tell us otherwise. Anything around communication between client and trainer?

Jordan (Client - Founder): A private message inbox inside the site, so they don't have to share emails right away. And for online sessions, it would be great if a Zoom meeting link was created automatically when they book.

Anil (Consultant): Automatic Zoom meetings would mean integrating with the Zoom API. That depends on Zoom account access and is likely custom work. Booking itself, do you need a calendar?

Jordan (Client - Founder): Not for launch. Messaging first, then they agree on times. The Zoom thing can be later if it's expensive.

Anil (Consultant): Noted. Reporting?

Jordan (Client - Founder): Trainers should see how many leads they got and how many they accepted. And I want an admin view of total leads per city per month.

Anil (Consultant): Is there existing data to migrate?

Jordan (Client - Founder): About eighty trainers in a Google Sheet. I'm not sure how many will join the paid plans though.

Anil (Consultant): We'll import those as unclaimed profiles and invite them. When do you want to launch?

Jordan (Client - Founder): January, to catch New Year's resolutions. That's fixed.

Anil (Consultant): Good. I'll put together the scope and estimate.
